ShiftReady VR

Site-specific clearance training: a site's own orientation rules become a short live shift. Runs on a laptop, or in a headset browser.

Open the site trainer

Laptop: drag to look and click what breaks a rule. Quest 3 (optional): open this page in the Meta Quest Browser and select Enter VR. Nothing to install.

How it works
  • The usual way: the trainee sees the site's orientation page, the dense document workers normally read before every new site, with a reading timer.
  • Turn it into training: each rule on the page lights up and flies to the spot on site where it applies: a crane lift, an open edge, a trench, a reversing truck, an excavator swing, a boom near power lines and a fenced protected area.
  • A live shift: each rule becomes a live situation. Spot it before time runs out. Some things look risky but follow the rules, and wrong picks count as false alarms.
  • Get cleared: spot a breach of every rule to be cleared for site entry. Missed rules come first next shift, and time windows tighten as skill grows.
  • Riley, a virtual site coach, explains each rule and answers questions about the page. Every shift can be exported as a CSV.

The orientation document SP-CON-012 and the site are fictional and written for demonstration only. Prototype by Mohammad Rezaeiashtiani.
