ShiftReady VR

Construction site hazard recognition training that runs in a headset browser or on a laptop.

Open the site trainer

Quest 3: open this page in the Meta Quest Browser and select Enter VR. Laptop: drag to look and click hazards. Nothing to install.

What happens in a shift
  • Rules from a site safety procedure turn into live situations on a busy construction site: a crane lift, an open edge on the top floor, a trench, a reversing truck, an excavator swing and a boom near power lines.
  • Spot each hazard before time runs out. Some workers look at risk but follow the rules, and wrong picks count as false alarms.
  • A learner profile adapts each shift: weaker rules come up more often and response windows tighten as skill grows.
  • Riley, a virtual safety coach, explains each rule and answers questions about the procedure.
  • Every shift can be exported as a CSV: reaction time, first look, false alarms and skill per rule.

The procedure SP-CON-012 used in the trainer is fictional and written for demonstration only. Prototype by Mohammad Rezaeiashtiani.
